# APP CONFIGURATION : ASSETS - TYPES OF ASSETS

## 1.Mechanical Equipment

**Name :** Mechanical Equipment
**Description :** Mechanical machinery used for industrial operations and maintenance.\
Examples: pumps, compressors, motors, fans, blowers, gearboxes, turbines.

## 2.Electrical Equipment

**Name :** Electrical Equipment
**Description :** Equipment used for electrical power generation, distribution, switching, and control.\
Examples: transformers, switchgear, generators, electrical panels, motor control centers, UPS systems.

## 3.Piping & Valves

**Name :** Piping & Valves
**Description :** Piping systems and components used to transport fluids, gases, steam, or other process media.\
Examples: pipelines, process pipes, valves, manifolds, flanges, pipe spools.

## 4.Vessels & Tanks

**Name :** Vessels & Tanks
**Description :** Equipment designed to contain, store, or process liquids, gases, or other materials.\
Examples: pressure vessels, storage tanks, reactors, boilers, silos, drums.

## 5.HVAC & Ventilation

**Name :** HVAC & Ventilation
**Description :** Equipment used for heating, ventilation, air conditioning, and temperature control.\
Examples: chillers, air handling units, exhaust fans, ventilation systems, cooling coils.

## 6.Instrumentation & Control

**Name :** Instrumentation & Control
**Description :** Devices and systems used to measure, monitor, automate, and control industrial processes.\
Examples: sensors, transmitters, analyzers, PLCs, DCS panels, control valves.

## 7.Production Machinery

**Name :** Production Machinery
**Description :** Machines used for manufacturing, processing, assembly, or packaging operations.\
Examples: CNC machines, presses, packaging machines, filling machines, assembly lines.

## 8.Material Handling Equipment

**Name :** Material Handling Equipment
**Description :** Equipment used to move, lift, transport, or position materials and goods.\
Examples: conveyors, cranes, hoists, forklifts, winches, pallet handling systems.

## 9.Utilities Equipment

**Name :** Utilities Equipment
**Description :** Equipment supporting essential utility services required for facility operations.\
Examples: air compressors, water treatment units, cooling towers, steam systems, industrial water pumps.

## 10.Fire & Safety Systems

**Name :** Fire & Safety Systems
**Description :** Equipment and systems designed to detect, prevent, or control fires and protect personnel.\
Examples: fire pumps, sprinkler systems, fire alarm panels, smoke detectors, fire suppression systems.

## 11.Civil & Structural Assets

**Name :** Civil & Structural Assets
**Description :** Buildings, structures, and civil infrastructure requiring inspection, repair, or maintenance.\
Examples: buildings, foundations, platforms, bridges, structural steel, roofs, walkways.

## 12.IT & Data Center Equipment

**Name :** IT & Data Center Equipment
\*\*Description :\*\* Information technology and supporting infrastructure equipment used in IT facilities and data centers.\
Examples: server racks, network cabinets, power distribution units, fiber distribution frames, data center cooling units.

## 13.Vehicles & Mobile Equipment

**Name :** Vehicles & Mobile Equipment
**Description :** Mobile machinery and vehicles used for transportation, construction, lifting, or industrial operations.\
Examples: trucks, excavators, mobile cranes, loaders, trailers, aerial work platforms.

## 14.Marine Equipment

**Name :** Marine Equipment
**Description :** Equipment used in ships, shipyards, offshore facilities, and marine operations.\
Examples: marine engines, winches, mooring equipment, cargo handling systems, gangways, davits.

## 15.Railway & Transport Equipment

**Name :** Railway & Transport Equipment
**Description :** Equipment and infrastructure components used in railway and transport operations.\
Examples: railway switches, signaling equipment, track machinery, rail maintenance vehicles, platform equipment.

## 16.Aviation & Airport Equipment

**Name :** Aviation & Airport Equipment
**Description :** Equipment used for aircraft servicing, airport operations, and ground handling.\
Examples: ground support equipment, baggage handling systems, aircraft towing tractors, boarding bridges.

## 17.Mining Equipment

**Name :** Mining Equipment
**Description :** Machinery and equipment used in mining, mineral extraction, and ore processing operations.\
Examples: crushers, drilling rigs, excavators, haul trucks, screening machines, conveyors.

## 18.Laboratory & Pharmaceutical Equipment

**Name :** Laboratory & Pharmaceutical Equipment
**Description :** Equipment used in laboratory testing, pharmaceutical manufacturing, and life sciences operations.\
Examples: laboratory instruments, autoclaves, bioreactors, centrifuges, mixing vessels, cleanroom equipment.

## 19.General Equipment

**Name :** General Equipment
**Description :** General-purpose equipment used for maintenance, workshop activities, and facility operations that does not fit another category.\
Examples: workbenches, workshop machinery, maintenance equipment, portable equipment.

## 20.Other

**Name :** Other
**Description :** Assets that do not fit any of the predefined asset type categories.\
Examples: specialized equipment or unique assets specific to a tenant or facility.
