# PERMIT CONFIGURATION - TYPES OF PERMIT

## 1. Hot Work Permit

Name: Hot Work Permit
Code: HOT
Description: Authorizes work that generates flames, sparks, heat, or other ignition sources in areas where fire or explosion hazards may exist. Examples: Welding, gas cutting, grinding, brazing, and other ignition-producing activities.
Color: Red · #DC2626

## 2. Cold Work Permit

Name: Cold Work Permit
Code: COLD
Description: Authorizes non-hot-work activities following hazard assessment and implementation of applicable safety precautions, operational controls, and required isolations. Examples: Routine mechanical maintenance, manual repairs, equipment inspection, and non-sparking maintenance activities.
Color: Green · #16A34A

## 3. Confined Space Permit

Name: Confined Space Permit ; Code: CSP ; Description: Controls entry into designated confined spaces where atmospheric, engulfment, access, or other serious hazards may exist. Examples: Tank entry, vessel inspection, and entry into designated pits or enclosed spaces; Color: Purple · #7C3AED

## 4. Electrical Permit

Name: Electrical Permit
Code: ELEC
Description: Controls electrical work involving hazards such as electric shock, arc flash, stored energy, and unexpected energization. Examples: Electrical maintenance, panel work, cable repair, and work on electrical equipment.
Color: Blue · #2563EB

## 5. Work at Height Permit

Name: Work at Height Permit
Code: WAH
Description: Controls work at elevated locations where a person could fall and suffer injury, according to the site's permit requirements. Examples: Roof maintenance, elevated structural repairs, and work on scaffolds or elevated platforms.
Color: Amber · #D97706

## 6. Excavation Permit

Name: Excavation Permit
Code: EXC
Description: Controls ground disturbance and excavation activities where buried services, ground instability, hazardous atmospheres, or other site hazards may exist. Examples: Trenching, foundation excavation, ground breaking, and underground utility investigation.; Color: Brown · #92400E
