## Spec

**V3-SPEC-01 closed.** [FR-LTO-103, line 446](/home/pradnyaanambrahm/Documents/Projects/great-ptw-loto-ai-app/docs/specs/2026-10-04-permitwiseai-v2-prd.md:446) requires a server-confirmed hold before reliance, Isolation completion, Issue or check-in. Hold acquisition and restoration exclude one another atomically, including while restoration is in progress. Offline evidence does not grant a hold. NFR-OFF-001 and criterion 8 agree.

**V3-SPEC-02 closed.** [FR-PTW-012, line 425](/home/pradnyaanambrahm/Documents/Projects/great-ptw-loto-ai-app/docs/specs/2026-10-04-permitwiseai-v2-prd.md:425) enforces approved end time on issue/reissue, check-in, progress and resume across statuses. It preserves device-time evaluation for offline submissions, approved reopening and close-out access. The midnight lapse applies only to a permit still Issued.

FR-CRW-002 requires a delay exceeding two hours. Keep that scenario accepted but remove the label assertion, or use receipt at 14:01 to exercise the late-sync threshold. FR-PTW-012 already retains the correct threshold.
