# Proposed corrections for the v0.3 review (v0.3 → v0.4)

Source: `v0.3-review.md` (3 findings, all verified valid). D-4 and D-5 are already decided by the owner (D27, D28) as the review recommends.

## VC-1 (V3-SPEC-01, P1) Holds are granted only by the server
- FR-LTO-103, replace the first bullet with: "Every hold, including the first on a point, is granted by the server before the permit relies on the point. The server grants a hold atomically and refuses it while a restoration of that point is requested or in progress. Offline, the app records the isolation and try-out evidence, but the hold is not taken: the permit's Isolation step cannot complete, and the permit cannot be issued or check crew in, until the server confirms the hold. The performer records the hold (and the permit's personal locks, FR-LTO-104), and a holder of "verify isolation" verifies the point (try-out) for that permit. The High clash of [v1 FR-SIM-021] must be resolved first."
- FR-LTO-103, replace the restoration bullet with: "A point can be physically restored only when no other permit holds it and no personal lock is on it. The server grants restoration atomically: a granted restoration request blocks new holds on the point until the restoration is verified or cancelled. The restoration screen lists any remaining holders and locks. Offline, the app shows "Connect to confirm no other holder before restoring" and records no restoration."
- NFR-OFF-001: replace "isolation steps except restoration (FR-LTO-103)" with "isolation steps (evidence recorded offline; holds and restoration need a server confirmation, FR-LTO-103)".
- §22: remove R8 (resolved by the requirement above).
- §21 criterion 8, append: "; a hold recorded offline does not count until the server confirms it, and simultaneous requests for a hold and a restoration on one point never both succeed".

## VC-2 (V3-SPEC-02, P1) The approved end time is enforced in every status
- Replace FR-PTW-012 with: "Expiry: a permit's work window ends at its approved end time, as extended or renewed. From that time, whatever the permit's status (Approved, Issued, Active or Suspended), the server refuses issue and reissue, check-in, progress and resume, checking the end time on every such action, not only by a timer. An Issued or Active permit becomes Expired; an Approved or Suspended permit keeps its status, marked "Past end time". Only an approved extension or renewal ([v1 FR-MDP-009]) reopens the window. Check-out, making safe, release and restoration of holds, incidents, the extension request and closure stay available, and the coordinator and safety officer are alerted until it is closed."
- FR-CRW-002 offline bullet: after "an in-limit reading within its validity (FR-GAS-002)" add "and refuses check-in after the permit's end time (FR-PTW-012)".
- §21, new criterion 12: "With the test clock, a noon-ending Issued permit and a noon-ending Suspended permit, at 14:00, refuse first check-in and resume until an extension is approved, while check-out and close-out actions keep working."

## VC-3 (V3-STD-01, P2) Receiving queued agency actions after access ends
- NFR-SEC-002: "exactly three paths" becomes "exactly four paths"; append: "(d) Late agency submissions: when an agency account's access to a client permit has ended (End now, wind-down finished, or the receiver duty handed over), the sync service, under server authority, appends each action that account queued for that permit, with its evidence files, to the permit's "Received after End now" list, unapplied. It accepts a submission only if the account was assigned to that permit (nominated receiver or crew member) when its access ended, and refuses all others. It grants no read of the client permit and no permit action; the agency device sees only "Delivered for review". Each submission is logged in both tenants."
- FR-AGY-008 End now bullet: after "kept, unapplied, in a "Received after End now" list" add "(through NFR-SEC-002 d)".
- §18 Isolation: name path (d) with the other three.
- §21 criterion 4, append: "; after End now, an action queued on an agency device reaches the client permit's review list through path (d), while the agency's ordinary reads and writes on that permit fail and submissions for permits the account was never assigned to are refused".
