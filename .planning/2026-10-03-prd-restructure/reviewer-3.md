Overall: The PRD faithfully carries D1–D23 and the structure is sound, but it is not ready for approval. Four problems would stop the build or the first pilot. The default workflow fails its own escalation check (R3-01). "Active plant" has two meanings, and routine changes such as adding a plant, a leaver or an engagement ending leave a live legal entity failing check 3 with no defined behaviour (R3-02). Health data is entered before consent can exist, which also excludes crews who never sign in (R3-03). The phase plan schedules check-in, mobile, consent and encryption after the phases whose exit criteria depend on them (R3-04). The majors are mostly about what happens on day 2 of a real job: poor signal at check-in, substitutes and an absent supervisor, an engagement or trial ending while a crew is on site, and how inherited updates and roles behave across the three levels. The design system needs no new tokens. The only gap is a diagram component, covered by R3-15. With the pasted text above applied, every requirement has an observable outcome and Phase 3 becomes a demonstrable pilot slice.
