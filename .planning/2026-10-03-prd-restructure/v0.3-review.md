## Standards

### V3-STD-01 — P2: Authorize post-revocation evidence receipt explicitly

**Location:** [FR-AGY-008, PRD line 157](/home/pradnyaanambrahm/Documents/Projects/great-ptw-loto-ai-app/docs/specs/2026-10-04-permitwiseai-v2-prd.md:157), [NFR-SEC-002, line 549](/home/pradnyaanambrahm/Documents/Projects/great-ptw-loto-ai-app/docs/specs/2026-10-04-permitwiseai-v2-prd.md:549), §18 Isolation (593).

After End now, agency access ends immediately, but queued agency actions must still be received into the client permit's "Received after End now" list. None of the three exhaustive cross-tenant paths authorizes that receipt: path (a) requires an active engagement or wind-down and held step permissions; path (b) exposes limited staff data; path (c) writes an agency checkout time. End now removes path (a)'s authority.

A trusted server rejection writer could implement this safely, so this is a missing authorization contract, not a demonstrated exploit or a claim that the behavior is impossible. Its narrow exception must be stated rather than leaving engineers to restore revoked access or silently discard evidence.

**Required correction:** Specify append-only receipt under server-controlled authority, validated against the recording account's historical assignment to that permit. It must grant no client reads or ordinary permit actions and apply none of the queued mutations automatically. Update the exhaustive path description and isolation acceptance scenarios consistently.

**Acceptance scenario:** Queue an action, perform End now, then reconnect the agency device. The evidence reaches the client's review list while ordinary agency reads and writes still fail; submissions for unrelated permits are refused.

## Spec

### V3-SPEC-01 — P1: Online restoration cannot see an offline hold

**Location:** [FR-LTO-103, PRD line 444](/home/pradnyaanambrahm/Documents/Projects/great-ptw-loto-ai-app/docs/specs/2026-10-04-permitwiseai-v2-prd.md:444), NFR-OFF-001 (562), acceptance criterion 8 (655), [R8, line 673](/home/pradnyaanambrahm/Documents/Projects/great-ptw-loto-ai-app/docs/specs/2026-10-04-permitwiseai-v2-prd.md:673).

Permit A holds a point on the server. Permit B records and verifies its hold offline, as the offline isolation requirements allow. A then requests restoration online before B syncs. The server sees no other holder and can authorize restoration while B relies on the point. B's earlier try-out verifies the point at that earlier time; it does not prevent A's later restoration. Personal locks are optional, so they cannot supply a universal guard.

R8 explicitly records this race, but its live-check mitigation cannot discover an unsynced hold. "Consider requiring a connection" leaves the mandatory no-restoration-while-another-permit-relies-on-it guarantee in FR-LTO-103 and criterion 8 unsatisfied.

**Required correction:** Make server-confirmed acquisition/reservation mandatory before a permit relies on a shared point, and ensure acquisition and restoration authorization exclude each other, including while restoration is in progress. Offline evidence recording must not establish an unregistered hold that authorizes work. Resolve R8 as a binding requirement before the shared-isolation capability is approved for implementation.

**Acceptance scenario:** Attempt B's hold acquisition offline while A requests restoration online. Either B already has a server-confirmed hold and A is blocked, or B is prevented from relying on the point. Test simultaneous online acquisition and restoration as well.

### V3-SPEC-02 — P1: Issued and Suspended permits can pass their end time without an entry/resume gate

**Location:** [FR-PTW-012, PRD line 424](/home/pradnyaanambrahm/Documents/Projects/great-ptw-loto-ai-app/docs/specs/2026-10-04-permitwiseai-v2-prd.md:424), FR-PTW-007 (395), FR-CRW-006 (409), FR-PTW-009 (421).

Expiry changes only an Active permit to Expired. A permit issued at 09:00 with an approved end time of 12:00 can remain Issued at 14:00: the new issue-lapse rule waits until the calendar date ends. Its first-check-in guards do not check the approved end time. Likewise, a permit suspended before noon can satisfy the stated gas, isolation, SIMOPS and crew checks and resume at 14:00 without an approved extension.

The carried-over FR-MDP-009 checks validity at day-transition; it does not close these same-day paths. The repaired open-close-out protections preserve cleanup access but do not prevent this unauthorized continuation of work.

**Required correction:** Enforce the approved end time across Issued, Active and Suspended states and on issue/reissue, check-in and resume, independently of a timer's current status update. Only an approved extension or renewal may reopen the work window. Preserve checkout, making safe and closure access after expiry.

**Acceptance scenario:** With the test clock, advance a noon-ending Issued permit and a noon-ending Suspended permit to 14:00. First check-in and resume must fail until the required extension/renewal is approved; close-out actions must continue to work.
