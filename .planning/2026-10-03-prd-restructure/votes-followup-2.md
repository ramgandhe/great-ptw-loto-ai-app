New conflicts:
- FX-1 × FX-2: FX-2 makes a non-last holder close by releasing its hold, confirmed by a holder of "verify isolation". FX-1's Closure part 2 needs only "verify restoration" and applies only to the last holder, so a release has no part, no role and no rule-check coverage, and nothing stops the requester from confirming their own release. Resolved by the FX-1 and FX-2 amendments.
- FX-1 × §9.1 check 3: removing Execution's parts removes the only check that someone at the plant holds "resume". A legal entity could then go live with stopped permits that nobody can resume, and they would stay Suspended. Resolved by the check 3 sentence in FX-1.
- FX-1 glossary: "one to three ordered parts" contradicts "Execution has no parts". Resolved by the FX-1 amendment.
- FX-2 × NFR-OFF-001 (exists in v0.2, made central by FX-2): an offline device cannot know whether another permit took a hold on the point since its last sync, so "the last holder restores" cannot be decided offline. Re-energising a point another crew relies on is the worst failure mode. Resolved by the FX-2 amendment (restoration needs a connection).
- FX-5 × offline queue: after End now, the agency's queued check-outs, stop-work events and incident reports are refused because access ended. Safety information would be lost, yet applying writes from a revoked party, with spoofable device times, is unsafe. Resolved by the quarantine list in the FX-5 amendment.
- FX-5 × FX-6 × FR-AGY-009: the internal receiver who takes over after End now must find agency crew on site. Snapshots hold names but no photos, and FX-6 opens only the emergency subset. Resolved by adding the photo in the FX-6 amendment.
- FX-6 privacy gap: under FR-PRV-006's unbounded "72 hours after an incident ... whatever the permit's status", an incident reported months after closure, with the engagement long over, would reopen cross-tenant access to agency staff's health data with no emergency purpose. Resolved by bounding the window in the FX-6 amendment. FX-6's "open close-out" and FR-PRV-006's "until Closed, Rejected or Cancelled" were also two definitions of the same window; the FX-6 amendment unifies them.
- FX-7 × §18 Mobile: "emergency contacts stay until the permit closes" contradicts the 7-day offline wipe and app lock. The 24-hour expiry is also measured on a device clock the user can set back. Resolved by the FX-7 amendment.
- FX-8 × shared devices and NFR-SEC-006: §18 refuses only a tenant mismatch. On a shared site tablet, after user 1 signs out, user 1's queued actions would replay under user 2's session whenever user 2 also has a person record in that tenant, so the audit names the wrong actor and FR-ROL-005 is evaluated on the wrong account. A background tenant-A session that fetches data while tenant B is displayed would also defeat NFR-SEC-006's clearing on switch. Resolved by the FX-8 amendment.
