New conflicts:
1. FX-1 conflicts with FR-CRW-006. Under FX-1, step parts run in the order listed. §5.4 puts "check crew in/out" in part 1 of Daily revalidation (Receiver), before part 2 (Safety officer). The crew would then be checked in before the safety officer revalidates, which FR-CRW-006 forbids. Fix: the first item of my FX-1 amendment.
2. FX-1 conflicts with a safe de-isolation order. In the ordered Closure step, the Receiver restores isolation in part 1, and the Coordinator only inspects the area (guards back, area clear) in part 3. Nothing confirms that guards are back and the area is clear before the equipment is re-energised. Fix: the Closure part 1 sentence in my FX-1 amendment.
3. FX-1 ("Execution has no parts") weakens check 3 and §9.2-1. No check then keeps a holder of "resume" or "record progress" at a plant, so a stopped permit could never resume, and check 3's Executor clause no longer applies to anything. Fix: the check 3 sentence in my FX-1 amendment.
4. FX-3 breaks when the first check-in falls on a later day than Issue (for example, issued on Friday, work starts on Monday). Either Friday's checks count for Monday, which skips revalidation, or revalidation cannot run, because Execution has not started (Issue part 2 is still open). That is the same cycle again. Fix: my FX-3 amendment, under which the issue lapses at the end of the day.
5. FX-3 and the Phase 4 exit (an offline check-in on day 3) conflict with NFR-OFF-001 and FR-CRW-002. Daily revalidation and the safety check are not offline actions, and an offline check-in checks only certificates and induction. So on day 3 the offline check-in is either impossible or bypasses FR-CRW-006 and FR-GAS-002. Fix: add "daily revalidation (both parts)" to the offline actions in NFR-OFF-001. Add to FR-CRW-002's offline bullet: "and refuses check-in unless the device holds that day's completed revalidation and, where gas testing applies, an in-limit reading within its validity (FR-GAS-002)".
6. FX-4 conflicts with the first bullet of FR-AGY-008. With FX-4, a permit not yet issued that holds isolation points (in the default workflow, Isolation comes before Approval) is both "not yet issued, so it returns to Raise" and "open close-out, so the agency winds down". Fix: my FX-4 amendment.
7. FX-5 (and a handover to an internal receiver under FR-PTW-013) conflicts with NFR-SEC-002 ("exactly two paths"), FR-AGY-010 and FR-CRW-007. When the client's internal receiver checks out agency crew, the agency's attendance record, which enforces one check-in at a time across clients, must be updated. That is a client-to-agency write, and no path allows it. The worker stays checked in and is blocked at every other client. Fix: add to NFR-SEC-002: "(c) A client receiver's check-out of an agency worker writes only the check-out time to that worker's agency attendance record, through one SECURITY DEFINER function, and is logged in both tenants."
8. FX-4 and FR-ONB-013 (trial expiry) conflict with FR-CRW-006. The read-only list of actions that "keep working" includes check-in but leaves out daily revalidation and handover. A multi-day permit with open close-out then either cannot check in on day 2, or skips revalidation. Fix: in the second bullet of FR-ONB-013, add "daily revalidation, handover" to the list of actions.
