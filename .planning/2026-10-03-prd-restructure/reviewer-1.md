# Reviewer 1: permit-to-work domain and process safety

Reviewed: `docs/specs/2026-10-04-permitwiseai-v2-prd.md` v0.1 against Notes2, decisions D1–D23 and `docs/specs/merged_prd.md` (v1).
New requirement IDs proposed below are numbered consistently across findings (FR-PTW-011 to 014, FR-CRW-007 to 009, FR-GAS-001 to 002, FR-LTO-103 to 104, FR-ROL-007 to 009).

### R1-01 [blocker] Safety steps can finish after the permit is issued, or not at all
- Where: FR-WFE-001; FR-WFD-006; §21 criterion 2
- Problem: The fixed safety order only forces Isolation before Execution. A legal entity can therefore place Safety check, Gas test or Check sheets after Execution starts, or Isolation after Issue, so the crew is briefed and checked in on live plant. A parallel group set to "first response" completes as soon as one step responds, so in §21 criterion 2 (Safety check and Gas test in parallel) the gas test can be skipped.
- Change: (1) In FR-WFE-001, replace "Isolation, when required, completes before Execution." with: "Every Check sheets, Safety check, Gas test and Isolation step that applies to the permit completes before Issue. Daily revalidation runs only inside Execution. Closure is last." (2) In FR-WFD-006, replace "A parallel group needs all of its steps, or the first response, as configured." with: "A parallel group made only of Approval steps needs all of its steps, or the first response, as configured [v1 FR-PTW-016]. A group that contains any other step type always needs all of its steps." (3) Add §9.1 check 12: "Every parallel group set to 'first response' contains only Approval steps."

### R1-02 [blocker] The crew side can switch safety steps off, and scope changes keep stale safety results
- Where: FR-PTW-003; FR-WFD-005; FR-WFD-008; §9.1 checks 8 and 9; FR-WFE-005; FR-WFE-006; FR-LST-001
- Problem: The receiver (the performing party) sets "isolation required" and "gas test required" and picks the hazards that set the risk level. Those facts decide whether the Isolation, Gas test and High-risk steps run, so under-declaring removes them. With LOTOTO switched off, check 8 forbids an Isolation step and check 9 no longer applies, so a permit marked isolation required runs with no isolation control at all. Safety check is optional (FR-WFD-005), even for High risk. FR-WFE-006 invalidates only approvals, so after the place, machine or hazards change, the old safety check, gas test and isolation verification still count.
- Change: (1) Add to FR-LST-001: "Each permit type and each hazard can set 'isolation required' and 'gas test required' (with the gas parameters to test). These are minimums for any permit that uses them." (2) In FR-PTW-003, after "isolation and gas-test needs" add: "Needs that the permit type or a chosen hazard implies are pre-set and cannot be cleared. The receiver, coordinator and safety officer can add needs. Only the safety officer can clear a need that someone added, and must give a reason." (3) Replace §9.1 check 9 with: "For every active permit type and every combination of conditions: isolation required meets an Isolation step (LOTOTO on) or a Safety check step (LOTOTO off); gas test required meets a Gas test step; multi-day meets a Daily revalidation step; risk level High meets a Safety check step. Each of these steps comes before Issue." Add to §11.1: "FR-LTO-105 While LOTOTO is off, the Safety check of an isolation-required permit records the outside isolation certificate number and who verified the isolation. Issue is refused without them." (4) Replace FR-WFE-006 with: "Before Issue, editing a scope field (permit type, hazards, place, machine, PPE, isolation and gas-test needs, start and end time, crew lead or agency) re-evaluates step conditions, invalidates every completed step after Receive, and restarts from the first of them. Administrative fields (for example contact numbers) do not. After Issue, scope fields are locked: to change scope, the permit is suspended and sent back, or a new permit is raised." Add to FR-WFE-005: "This applies only when no scope field changed."

### R1-03 [blocker] A permit can end with isolations applied and people still on the job
- Where: FR-WFE-003 (reject); §10.5 (Cancelled, Expired); v1 FR-INC-011 (accident cancels the permit, carried over in §11.4); FR-PTW-010; FR-CRW-001; v1 FR-PTW-010
- Problem: In the default order Isolation comes before Approval, so when an approver rejects, the permit "ends" with locks on and no way to restore them. The automatic cancel after an accident, and expiry, can likewise end a permit with crew checked in and isolations applied. Closure needs only "isolation restored where applied": it does not require every crew member to be checked out, and v1 FR-PTW-010 (safety check before closure) was dropped without a decision. Nobody can see who is on site right now for an emergency headcount, and one person can be checked in on two permits at once.
- Change: Add FR-PTW-011: "Making safe: once a permit has reached Isolation or Issue, it cannot become Closed, Rejected or Cancelled until (a) every crew member is checked out, or the receiver records where each one is, and (b) every isolation applied under it is restored and verified, or the coordinator transfers it to another open permit and the safety officer verifies the transfer. Reject, cancel and an accident stop work at once and block any further step; the permit gets a 'make safe' task for the receiver and coordinator, and its final status is set when that task is done." Add FR-PTW-012: "Expiry: when an Active permit reaches its end time, progress and check-in stop and the permit becomes Expired. Check-out, isolation restoration, extension and closure stay available, and the coordinator and safety officer are alerted until it is closed." Replace FR-PTW-010 with: "At completion the receiver reports the work done and confirms every crew member is out and checked out. Isolation is restored and verified where it was applied. The coordinator inspects the area (work area clear, guards and barriers back in place, equipment safe to return to service), records the result and accepts closure [v1 FR-PTW-010]. Closure is refused while any crew member is checked in or any isolation under the permit is not restored." Add FR-CRW-007: "A person can be checked in on only one permit at a time. Each plant has a live headcount (who is checked in, on which permit, at which place) for its coordinators, safety officers and admins; on mobile it is available offline as of the last sync. At the permit's end time each day, anyone still checked in is flagged to the receiver, coordinator and safety officer."

### R1-04 [blocker] Restoring one permit's isolation can re-energise another permit's work
- Where: §11.1 (FR-LTO-101, FR-LTO-102); v1 FR-SIM-021; NFR-OFF-001
- Problem: v1 FR-SIM-021 only flags two permits on one energy source as a High clash. Once that clash is resolved both permits go ahead, and whichever closes first restores the shared isolation while the other crew is still working. Crew members have no personal lock, so restoration does not wait for everyone to be clear. Offline, a device can record both perform and verify for the same point by one account before the server refuses it.
- Change: Add FR-LTO-103: "An isolation point can be held by more than one open permit. For each applied isolation point the platform records every permit that relies on it. Restoring the point is refused while any other permit that relies on it is not Closed, and the restoration screen lists those permits." Add FR-LTO-104: "A LOTOTO procedure can require personal locks. Each crew member records lock-on (with the lock number) at check-in and lock-off at check-out. Check-out is refused while that person's lock is on, and restoration is refused while any personal lock is on. Removing another person's lock needs the safety officer, a reason, and a record that the person was contacted or confirmed off site." Add to FR-LTO-102: "Offline, the device refuses a verify by the account that recorded perform on the same point, and the server checks again at sync. The same rule applies to restoring a point and verifying the restoration."

### R1-05 [blocker] The default escalation fails its own rule check and hands permit decisions to an admin
- Where: §8.5 (default escalation); §9.1 check 4; FR-WFD-010; FR-WFE-001; FR-ONB-003; v1 FR-ROL-003
- Problem: Level 1 of the default escalation goes "to the same role at the plant", which check 4 forbids. The platform default workflow, which every new organisation starts from (FR-ONB-003), therefore cannot pass its own checks. Level 2 goes to the legal-entity admin, which FR-WFD-010 does not allow (it allows a role at the plant or a named person), and it implies an admin acts on an approval. v1 FR-ROL-003 (admins technically barred from approve, reject and close) is dropped without a decision. The owner's parked decision NI-04 (whether an admin may override closure, due 2026-09-30) is not recorded.
- Change: (1) Replace §9.1 check 4 with: "No escalation level goes only to the person who has taken the step. An escalation to the step's own role is allowed at level 1 only: it releases the step from the person who took it and offers it again to every holder at the plant." (2) In §8.5, replace the escalation sentence with: "Default escalation: after the target, level 1 releases the step to its own role at the plant; after twice the target, level 2 flags the permit to the legal-entity admins as blocked (FR-WFD-010)." (3) Add FR-ROL-007: "Admin roles carry no permit permissions. An admin who receives a blocked-permit flag can reassign the step to another person who holds the step's role at the plant, or change role assignments. An admin can act on a permit step only through a permit role they hold at that plant. The API enforces this on every permit action [v1 FR-ROL-003]." (4) Add to §22: "D-4 | NI-04: may an organisation or legal-entity admin override permit closure? This PRD assumes not (FR-WFE-001, FR-ROL-007). | Product | Phase 3".

### R1-06 [blocker] Separation of duties misses the issuer/receiver split and is not checked before go-live
- Where: FR-ROL-005; FR-ROL-006; FR-AGY-007; FR-ONB-007; §9.1 check 3; §9.2; D19
- Problem: Nothing stops a person who holds both Coordinator and Receiver at a plant from raising, receiving and accepting closure of their own permit. That defeats the split between the person who issues and the person who does the work, which PTW depends on; D19's two pairs leave this concrete gap. The safety officer can also be the approver, which loses v1's independent safety gate (v1 FR-PTW-029). Go-live needs only one holder per role, so a plant where one person holds every role passes and then escalates every permit. An admin can assign themself any role. Roles are editable, so the Receiver role, which agency supervisors get by nomination, can be given approve, issue or verify isolation.
- Change: (1) Replace FR-ROL-005 with: "Segregation of duties, always enforced while a permit runs. On one permit, the same person cannot: (a) raise and approve; (b) approve and issue; (c) act as coordinator (raise, issue, accept substitute, accept closure) and as receiver (accept permit, check crew in or out, report completion); (d) perform the safety check and approve; (e) perform and verify the same isolation point (FR-LTO-102). An action taken on someone's behalf (FR-ROL-006) counts for both people. A step offered only to barred people escalates at once to its next level." (2) Append to §9.1 check 3: "At every active plant, each pair in FR-ROL-005 can be filled by two different people." (3) Add §9.2 check 3: "A role that agency staff can hold (FR-AGY-007) contains none of: approve, defer, send back, reject, issue permit, safety check, verify isolation, accept substitute, accept closure, decide near miss, review SIMOPS conflict." (4) Add FR-ROL-008: "An admin cannot change their own plant role assignments. Another LEGAL_ORG_ADMIN of the legal entity, or a TENANT_ORG_ADMIN, makes the change."

### R1-07 [blocker] Ending an engagement strands crew and isolations on live permits
- Where: FR-AGY-008; FR-AGY-004 ("the client can end it at any time"); NFR-SEC-002
- Problem: When an engagement ends, by end date or by the client, the agency loses access at once. That includes the receiver of a permit that is Issued, Active or Suspended, who can then no longer check crew out, report completion, restore isolations they applied (Receiver performs isolation by default, §8.2) or report an incident. Flagging the coordinator does not take over those duties.
- Change: Replace FR-AGY-008 with: "When an engagement ends (by end date or client action), no new permits can be assigned to the agency, and its permits that are not yet issued return to Raise for a new crew lead. On its permits that are Issued, Active or Suspended, no new check-ins are allowed. The agency's receiver and checked-in crew keep access to those permits only, for check-out, completion, isolation restoration and incident reporting, until each permit is closed or the coordinator hands the receiver duty to an internal receiver (FR-PTW-013). The coordinator and safety officer are alerted until no such permit remains. Records of work already done stay with the permits." Add to NFR-SEC-002: "...or by the open permits of an ended engagement under FR-AGY-008."

### R1-08 [major] Stop-work authority can be configured away, and resume has no re-check
- Where: FR-PTW-009; FR-ROL-001 ("suspend", "resume"); v1 FR-ROL-002; NFR-OFF-001
- Problem: "Suspend" is an ordinary permission, so a legal entity can take it away from the safety officer and the crew. After Issue the safety officer has no gate left in which to refuse, so v1 FR-ROL-002 (the safety officer can cancel at any status before Closed) is lost without a decision. "Follow v1 behaviour" cannot be tested, because v1 does not say what must be re-checked before work resumes. A device that is offline does not learn that the server has suspended the permit, and keeps recording progress.
- Change: Replace FR-PTW-009 with: "Stop work: any checked-in crew member, the receiver, the coordinator, the safety officer or the approver of a permit can stop work on it at any time. The permit becomes Suspended at once, and the permit's coordinator, receiver and safety officer are notified. Stop work is not a role-editor permission and cannot be removed. The safety officer can also cancel the permit at any status before Closed [v1 FR-ROL-002]; FR-PTW-011 then applies. A holder of 'resume' can resume only after: the reason for stopping is answered (with an incident recorded where one occurred); a holder of 'verify isolation' confirms the isolations are intact; where gas testing applies, a gas test passes within its validity (FR-GAS-002); SIMOPS is re-evaluated; and the crew is checked in again." Add to NFR-OFF-001: "An action recorded offline on a permit that the server had already suspended, cancelled or expired at the action's device time is kept, marked, and flagged to the coordinator and safety officer at sync."

### R1-09 [major] Gas readings go stale, and each day's crew is checked in before revalidation
- Where: FR-LST-001 (gas-test parameters); §8.5 (Gas test before Approval); FR-CRW-006; FR-MDP-101; §8.2 (Daily revalidation)
- Problem: In the default order the gas test is recorded before Approval. Add the approval and issue targets and any escalations, and the reading can be hours old when the crew goes in. The PRD defines no validity period, no re-test before entry or after a break, and no response to a reading outside its limits. FR-CRW-006 makes the day's check-in record part of the daily revalidation, which reads as the crew being checked in (on site) before that day's safety revalidation.
- Change: Add FR-GAS-001: "Each gas-test parameter in the lists carries limits and a validity period (defaults come from the industry library). A permit type can require continuous monitoring." Add FR-GAS-002: "Issue, the first check-in of each day and resume after a suspension are refused unless every required parameter has an in-limit reading, taken at the work place within its validity period. A reading outside its limits on an Issued or Active permit stops work at once (FR-PTW-009) and alerts the safety officer and coordinator." Replace FR-CRW-006 and FR-MDP-101 with: "On each day of a multi-day permit, the receiver and safety officer first complete the daily revalidation (conditions, isolations intact, gas test under FR-GAS-002, SIMOPS re-evaluated). Only then can the receiver check crew in. The day's crew list and check-in record are then attached to that day's revalidation record."

### R1-10 [major] No handover of coordinator or receiver duties between shifts or days
- Where: FR-WFE-002; FR-PTW-002; FR-CRW-001; §11.3; Notes2 Agencies #8
- Problem: The first person to act takes a step, and the first agency supervisor to accept becomes the permit's receiver. Nothing lets a live permit pass to the next shift's coordinator or receiver. Multi-day agency work, which Notes2 says may have different staff each day, then depends on one person being there every day. Shift handover is a known weak point in PTW and must be explicit and acknowledged.
- Change: Add FR-PTW-013: "Handover: on a permit that is Issued, Active or Suspended, the person who holds the coordinator or receiver duty can hand it to another person who holds the same role at the plant (for an agency, another receiver nominated on the engagement). The handover includes a note on the state of the work, the isolations and the crew. The incoming person must accept before the outgoing person is released; until then both are listed. A receiver handover also needs the coordinator's acceptance. When the holder is absent, the coordinator or a LEGAL_ORG_ADMIN can reassign the duty, giving a reason. Every handover is recorded on the permit and shown in the work queues of both people." Add "hand over permit duty" to the FR-ROL-001 list.

### R1-11 [major] Competence is checked only for crew certificates, not for role holders, site induction or briefing
- Where: FR-CRW-003; FR-CRW-004; FR-PTW-007; FR-ROL-003; FR-WFE-002; Notes2 Human resources #4
- Problem: Notes2 requires people to meet the eligibility for the role they carry out, yet anyone assigned a role can approve, test gas or verify isolation without any authorisation or certificate check (competence, ISO 45001 clause 7.2). Agency staff can be checked in with no site induction for the plant. Briefing is one tick by the receiver at Issue, so a substitute or a new crew member on a later day is checked in without being briefed and without acknowledging it themselves.
- Change: Add FR-ROL-009: "A role can list required certificates or competencies (for example 'authorised gas tester' for a role that holds 'record gas test'). Pickers (FR-ROL-003) and step offers (FR-WFE-002) include only holders whose required certificates are valid that day, and an action by a holder whose certificate has expired is refused. §9.3 warns when no holder of a role at a plant has a valid required certificate." Add FR-CRW-008: "A plant can require a site induction with a validity period, recorded per person. Check-in is refused without a valid induction for that plant." Add FR-CRW-009: "Before a person's first check-in on a permit (including substitutes and new crew on later days), the receiver briefs them on the permit's hazards, controls, PPE and isolations, and the person acknowledges the briefing on the device in their own name. Check-in is refused without that acknowledgement."

### R1-12 [major] SIMOPS runs on fewer events than v1, and before the machine is known
- Where: FR-PTW-005; v1 FR-SIM-011, 015, 016, 017, 021 (carried over in §11.2)
- Problem: FR-PTW-005 runs SIMOPS "at submission" and when place, time or hazards change. That drops the triggers in v1 FR-SIM-016 (machine edits, extension, multi-day continuation, nightly re-evaluation), yet §11.2 carries FR-SIM-016 over, so the two contradict each other. "Submission" is not defined in v2, and the machine and hazards that v1 FR-SIM-011 compares are added only at Receive. v1 FR-SIM-015's "before either permit may proceed" has no gate in v2.
- Change: Replace FR-PTW-005 with: "SIMOPS evaluation runs when Receive completes; on every change to place, machine, isolation points, hazards, permit type or times; on extension or renewal; at the start of each day of a multi-day permit; at Issue; and nightly for permits not yet Active [v1 FR-SIM-016, FR-SIM-021]. While a Medium or High clash involving a permit is unresolved, that permit cannot be issued [v1 FR-SIM-015]. If the other permit is already Issued or Active, the newer permit is frozen at its current step [v1 FR-SIM-017]."

### R1-13 [major] The fixed permission list cannot express cancel, extension, renewal or restoration checks
- Where: FR-ROL-001; §10.5 (Cancelled, Expired); §9.1 check 11; v1 FR-MDP-009, FR-LTO-012, FR-INC-009; v1 §3.2 (issuer requests extensions and accepts the day's progress)
- Problem: Cancelled is a status, and v1's extension, renewal (FR-MDP-009), controlled restoration and incident closure are all carried over. The fixed list has no permission for cancel, extend, renew, verify restoration, accept a day's progress, acknowledge a Low SIMOPS clash or close an incident. These actions cannot be given to any role, and check 11 cannot check them.
- Change: Append to the FR-ROL-001 list: "cancel permit; request extension; approve extension; renew permit; accept day's progress; verify restoration; acknowledge Low SIMOPS conflict; close incident; hand over permit duty". Add to the §5.2 default duties: Coordinator: cancel permit before Issue, request extension, accept day's progress, acknowledge Low SIMOPS conflict; Approver: approve extension, renew permit, cancel permit; Safety officer: verify restoration, close incident, cancel permit. Add FR-PTW-014: "Cancel: the coordinator can cancel a permit before Issue. After Issue, only the approver or the safety officer can cancel it, and must give a reason. Cancelling follows FR-PTW-011."

### R1-14 [major] Emergency data disappears at the moment of an accident
- Where: FR-PRV-006; v1 FR-INC-011 (carried over in §11.4); D7
- Problem: The emergency subset is visible only while the permit is Issued, Active or Suspended, but an accident cancels the permit automatically (v1 FR-INC-011). Blood group and health conditions therefore vanish during the emergency response. Crew who apply isolation before Issue (default order) are on site with no emergency data visible. The receiver never sees it, though on a night shift the receiver is often the only permit role with the crew. This challenges D7's timing and audience on safety grounds only.
- Change: Replace FR-PRV-006 with: "The emergency subset (blood group, emergency contacts, health conditions the person listed) is visible to the coordinator, safety officer and receiver of a permit, only for people on that permit. It is visible from the start of the permit's first Isolation step or its Issue, whichever comes first, until the permit is Closed, Rejected or Cancelled. After an incident on that permit is reported, it stays visible for 72 hours whatever the permit's status. Every view is logged (FR-PRV-008)."

### R1-15 [major] Trial expiry can lock out live work and delete safety records
- Where: FR-ONB-013; FR-AUD-003; FR-PRV-009
- Problem: In read-only mode "nothing new can be raised", but the PRD does not say whether check-out, stop work, incident reports, isolation restoration and closure still work on live permits. Once the organisation is suspended only admins can sign in, which strands crew and isolations on any open permit. Deleting all data 90 days later contradicts FR-AUD-003 (audit kept for the safety-record period) and FR-PRV-009 (permit records kept 10 years), with no stated exception and no export.
- Change: Replace FR-ONB-013 with: "If the trial ends without a plan, the organisation becomes read-only for 30 days: no new permits can be raised or issued. On permits already issued, check-in and check-out, stop work, progress, incidents, isolation restoration and closure keep working. The organisation is suspended only once no permit is Issued, Active or Suspended and no isolation is applied; until then the read-only period is extended and the admins are told why. While the organisation is suspended, only admins can sign in, to choose a plan or to export. During read-only and suspension, admins can export all permit, LOTOTO, SIMOPS, incident and audit records at any time. Data is deleted 90 days after suspension; this deletion is the only exception to FR-AUD-003 and FR-PRV-009. Admins are emailed 7 days and 1 day before each change."

Overall: The PRD carries Notes2 and D1–D23 faithfully into structure, tenancy, agencies, roles and configuration, and it is clear and mostly testable. As a permit-to-work control system it is not ready for approval. Making the workflow and roles configurable opens bypasses that v1's fixed flow did not have: safety steps can run after Issue or be skipped by "first response" groups (R1-01) or by flags the crew side sets (R1-02), separation of duties has gaps (R1-06), admins can be handed permit decisions (R1-05), and a permit can end, or an engagement or trial can lapse, with crew on site and isolations applied (R1-03, R1-07, R1-15) or a shared isolation restored under another crew (R1-04). All 15 changes are additive requirement text or rule checks and fit the owner's decisions. Only R1-14 touches a decision (D7), on safety grounds. Before build, also worth settling: a status-to-step mapping for configurable workflows (§10.5 does not say which status a permit has during Safety check, Gas test or Isolation), cross-plant SIMOPS for plants that share a site (FR-SIM-102), and a way to withdraw a workflow version found unsafe while permits are still running on it (FR-WFD-016).
